Why Expense Tracking Alone Doesn't Build Wealth

Most people use finance apps only to look backward at what they spent on coffee or groceries. While expense tracking prevents overspending, wealth is built by tracking your Net Worth (everything you own minus everything you owe) and your Savings Rate (the percentage of income you keep).
